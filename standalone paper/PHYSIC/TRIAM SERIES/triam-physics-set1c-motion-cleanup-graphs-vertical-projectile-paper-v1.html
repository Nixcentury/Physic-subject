Reference Sheet / สูตรอ้างอิงที่อนุญาตให้ใช้
TRIAM SERIES Set 1C — Motion Cleanup

1. Uniform Acceleration

$v=u+at$
$s=ut+\dfrac12at^2$
$v^2=u^2+2as$

2. Position Equation

$x=x_0+ut+\dfrac12at^2$
$y=y_0+u_yt+\dfrac12a_yt^2$
delayed start: $(t-t_0)$

3. Graphs

slope of $x-t$ $=v$
slope of $v-t$ $=a$
area under $v-t$ $=\Delta x$

4. Average

average speed $=\dfrac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}$
average velocity $=\dfrac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$

5. Vertical Motion

upward positive: $a=-g$
top point: $v=0$, $a=-g$
$g=10\text{ m/s}^2$

6. Projectile

horizontal: $x=u_xt$
vertical: $y=y_0+u_yt-\dfrac12gt^2$
$v=\sqrt{v_x^2+v_y^2}$
