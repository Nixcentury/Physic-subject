Short Answer Key / เฉลยสั้น

Q1: Mass decreases because \(CO_2\) escapes. At 120 s, mass lost \(=0.30\,g\). At 300 s, mass lost \(=0.44\,g\).
Q2: \(0-60s\): \(0.18/60=0.0030\,g/s\). \(240-300s\): \(0.02/60=0.00033\,g/s\). Early interval is faster.
Q3: \(n_{HCl,0}=0.0200\,mol\). \(n_{CO_2}=0.30/44.0=0.00682\,mol\). \(n_{HCl\,used}=0.01364\,mol\). \([HCl]_{120s}=0.0636\,M\).
Q4: \(\Delta[HCl]=-0.1364\,M\). \(-\Delta[HCl]/\Delta t=0.00114\,M/s\). \(rate=0.000568\,M/s\).
Q5: \(60-120s=0.0020\,g/s\). \(120-180s=0.00133\,g/s\). Rate decreases over time because reactant concentration decreases and effective collision frequency decreases.
