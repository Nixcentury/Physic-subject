Torque depends on force, distance from pivot, and angle.

line through pivot: \(\tau=0\)
CCW \(+\) CW \(-\) Unit: \(N\cdot m\)

2. Rotational equilibrium / สมดุลการหมุน

\(\sum \tau=0\)
\(\sum F_x=0\)
\(\sum F_y=0\)

For a beam/see-saw problem, choose the pivot first, then sum clockwise and counterclockwise torque.
